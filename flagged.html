2. Limitations for generating picks

    2a. Derived estimates — our own lines (labeled, low confidence)

    For matches with no per-match market but with both teams carrying tournament-futures prices in the same event, we originate a line with the transparent ratio model p(A) = f(A) / (f(A) + f(B)) where f is the futures mid. These are not book lines. They are listed here so they can be reviewed and removed if the model is not wanted; inputs and links are shown on each match card on All Picks.
