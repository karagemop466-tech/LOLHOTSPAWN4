Standing Irregularities & Operational Flags

  • Provisional Tier Metadata (WAITING FOR INFORMATION): Over 65% of Hotspawn LoL lobby fixtures currently display Tier: WAITING FOR INFORMATION. In our pipeline, we do not guess or infer tournament tiers. They are preserved verbatim and flagged for ongoing verification.
  • External Market Coverage Gap: Established sportsbooks (such as Pinnacle, bet365, BetOnline) and prediction markets (Polymarket) only trade major tier-1/tier-2 leagues. Low-tier matches (e.g. PRM 3rd Division, Tunisian Super League) receive zero commercial pricing, necessitating our originated Elo model or a 50/50 baseline.
  • Sandbox Environment Egress: The local development sandbox enforces strict firewall rules (allowing only GitHub, NPM, and PyPI). Live production scraping is executed in GitHub Actions runners (ubuntu-latest) on a 6-hour schedule.
  • Informational Hotspawn Crowd %: While Hotspawn shows community pick percentages on cards (e.g. 90% picked), this is strictly treated as informational context and is never used as an input to win probability.
  • Game-1 markets as series proxy: For some series (e.g. Galions vs Solary, EMEA Masters Bo5) Polymarket lists only a Game 1 winner market, not a series/moneyline market. The Game-1 price is used as the best available external signal and is labeled “Game-1 market only” in the row evidence. A Game-1 favorite is not always the series favorite — treat these as lower confidence.
  • Start-time discrepancies (Hotspawn vs markets): Hotspawn's scheduled start times for several Worlds 2026 group matches differ from the market-listed start times by 1–4 hours (e.g. T1 vs LØS: Hotspawn 23:00Z vs Polymarket “6:00 PM ET” = 22:00Z, verified on the Polymarket event page 2026-10-21). Matches are joined on team names with a same-day time window; the Hotspawn time is shown on the board because picks lock against it.
  • Verified team-name alias (LØS = LOS): Hotspawn spells a Worlds 2026 team “LØS” (Nordic spelling) while Polymarket (event lol-los-t1-2026-10-23, page verified) and Kalshi (market KXLOL-WORLDS26-LOS) both list it as “LOS”. This alias is applied in name matching; the underlying match is unambiguous (same tournament, same opponent, same date).
  • Locked-out fixtures are excluded, not dropped: Hotspawn keeps some fixtures in “scheduled” status after their pick lock time has passed. Those are no longer available picks, so they are removed from the picks board and listed in Excluded Fixtures (Locked Out) below for audit.
  • Stale data handling: If any source fetch fails on a refresh, the pipeline reuses the previous snapshot and the site shows a STALE DATA banner (plus the failed sources here in debug) instead of inventing values.
  • Disqualified Team Still Open in Lobby (#1059956 — Solid Gaming vs DMG Esports, Komplettligaen, Oct 22 15:00Z): Official Leaguepedia Data confirms Solid Gaming was removed from the league after Round 2 (|dqteams=Solid Gaming) and their Oct 22 match vs DMG NO is an official 0–2 forfeit win (ff=1, winner=2) for DMG Esports, yet 67% of Hotspawn users picked Solid Gaming.
  • Already-Played & Rescheduled Ghost Duplicates in Pick'em Lobby: #1079143 (Pegasus Esports vs Team Reds, Arabian League 2nd Div, listed Oct 25) was already played on Oct 10 in Week 2 (Team Reds won 2–1); #1079026 (Reveal Multigaming vs ex-A41G, PRM 2nd Div, listed Oct 23) was already played early on Oct 20 after ex-A41G renamed to reGOLD eSport (0–2); and #1079024 (Phong Vũ Buffalo vs 404 Multigaming e.V., Oct 23) is a pre-reschedule duplicate of #1082746 (Nov 3), where Swiss team PvB Nation is also misattributed to defunct Vietnamese VCS team Phong Vũ Buffalo.
