Standing Irregularities & Operational Flags

  • Provisional Tier Metadata (WAITING FOR INFORMATION): Over 65% of Hotspawn LoL lobby fixtures currently display Tier: WAITING FOR INFORMATION. In our pipeline, we do not guess or infer tournament tiers. They are preserved verbatim and flagged for ongoing verification.
  • External Market Coverage Gap: Established sportsbooks (such as Pinnacle, bet365, BetOnline) and prediction markets (Polymarket) only trade major tier-1/tier-2 leagues. Low-tier matches (e.g. PRM 3rd Division, Tunisian Super League) receive zero commercial pricing, necessitating our originated Elo model or a 50/50 baseline.
  • Sandbox Environment Egress: The local development sandbox enforces strict firewall rules (allowing only GitHub, NPM, and PyPI). Live production scraping is executed in GitHub Actions runners (ubuntu-latest) on a 6-hour schedule.
  • Informational Hotspawn Crowd %: While Hotspawn shows community pick percentages on cards (e.g. 90% picked), this is strictly treated as informational context and is never used as an input to win probability.
