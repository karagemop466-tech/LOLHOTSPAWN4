THE HONEST BOTTOM LINE

Coverage ≠ confidence.

No source, no number. A missing market is not a 50/50 prediction. Amateur results, team aliases, unknown timezones and inaccessible sources cannot be filled by inference.

Fallback is a model, not a fact. The small, partial organizer archive is independently sourced, but there is no out-of-sample calibration. Equal model rates produce no arbitrary team-A pick.
