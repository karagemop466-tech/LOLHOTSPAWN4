LEAGUE OF LEGENDS / SOURCE-BACKED ANALYSIS

Methodology & sources

Trace every number. Understand every assumption. See every failed source, too.

What the numbers mean

  1. Inventory only: the public Hotspawn LoL fixtures response defines the complete list of unique IDs, teams, competition and listed UTC start/lock time. Crowd percentages, pick counts and Hotspawn results are dropped before caching or modeling.
  2. Bookmaker first: Pinnacle public LoL league discovery, parent pending/non-live matchup metadata and open full-series moneylines are joined by exact matchup ID. Required price scope: moneyline, period 0, key s;0;m, home and away. Map, handicap, correct-score and outright markets are rejected.
  3. Prediction market second: paginated Polymarket LoL events are searched for active, open, order-accepting Match Winner markets with sportsMarketType=moneyline, two named team outcomes and an explicit game/event start. child_moneyline and Game 1 are rejected even when prices happen to equal the series market.
  4. Exact match: both team names must agree after typography/case normalization, with the same competition family, a unique inventory match and explicit starts within six hours. No fuzzy team/academy aliases. Any nonidentical start remains prominently flagged; quoted and inventory times are both retained. Unknown or distant timestamps fail closed.
  5. No accepted market: only the independently dated organizer/community-wiki history model below may provide a fallback. If either team lacks eligible inputs, both probabilities and the pick stay null. An evidence-based equal estimate has no directional pick.

Other original sportsbook/esportsbook pages (GG.bet, Thunderpick, bwin) and organizer results (Komplett/RIVALS) are retried for access and listed for manual review. Restricted pages and client shells supply no accepted prices or results. This is not exhaustive bookmaker discovery, and access failure never means “market absent.”

Reproduce an external line

For American odds x, implied chance is 100/(x+100) when positive, or −x/(−x+100) when negative. Remove the two-way overround: pA = qA/(qA+qB), pB = 1−pA. Polymarket's paired outcome prices are normalized by their sum.

Each card retains the original publisher order, any reversal into Hotspawn order, raw quotes, both source URLs, observation time and provider-update time where supplied. Pinnacle does not supply a quote-change timestamp in this endpoint; retrieval time is not mislabeled as one. The first eligible bookmaker is primary; otherwise the prediction market is primary. Other evidence is displayed, not blindly averaged. Picks choose the strictly greater probability; ties never default to the first team.

These are normalized price implications, not a claim about the true chance of winning or profitability.

The independent fallback — and its limits

Model version: causal-same-format-series-log5-beta2-shrink10-v2. Uses only source-linked completed series for the same competition, season and BO1/BO3/BO5 format. Prime League uses the confirmed 2026/27 BO3 division context; other supported competitions require a fresh exact-team fixture in a recognized native community-wiki schedule. No team-code aliases are guessed. Separate groups require a stated common pool or connected observed opponents. It does not use seed ratings, Hotspawn crowd/history, undated team-profile records, fan predictions, future scores or championship-futures ratios.

  1. Require at least two causal completed series for each exact team, in the trailing 60 days and matching series format. Duplicate reports of the same dated pair count once (conservative for same-day doubleheaders). The last confirmed result of each team must be within 14 days of the build. Profiles, similarly named academy teams and unconfirmed aliases cannot satisfy this requirement.
  2. With a Beta(2,2) prior, calculate each partial record's smoothed series rate: r = (wins + 2)/(series + 4).
  3. Apply the log5 matchup formula: q = (rA − rA×rB)/(rA + rB − 2×rA×rB).
  4. Conservatively shrink the edge toward one half: w = min(nA,nB)/(min(nA,nB)+10); pA = 0.5 + w×(q−0.5), pB = 1−pA. No input history means no estimate, not a neutral fill.

Assumptions, not verified facts: Beta(2,2), log5 and the shrinkage constant 10 are fixed, transparent model choices, not fitted or validated parameters. The partial audited archive can be selection-biased; community wikis are not organizer endorsements and may contain corrections; it is not a complete season record. There is no strength-of-schedule, roster, draft or patch adjustment and no out-of-sample calibration. Treat all estimates as low-confidence illustrations, especially far-future fixtures.

No invented UTC: some organizer pages print a local date/time without a confirmed timezone. Native wiki timezone/DST fields are used only when explicit and recognized; they establish scheduled starts, not actual completion times. The training eligibility rule uses the next UTC day plus 20 hours as an intentionally late bound (timezone/series buffer), and requires it to be before both build time and fixture start. This is a modeling cutoff rule, not the actual completion timestamp.

Automatic source restriction: newly parsed organizer result headers with no confirmed season are retained as observations but cannot satisfy same-season training eligibility. Audited observations remain dated observations. If the runner cannot retrieve new organizer results, the archive does not pretend to refresh; the last-result freshness rule eventually removes estimates. The native community-wiki collector retries recognized Data: pages every refresh, replaces corrected page observations, and never expands series/standings into invented individual games, timestamps or league-average opponents. Administrative outcomes (including the separately disputed Solid Gaming rows) are excluded. If access or template validation fails, the original receipt date is preserved. No manual maintenance is required to stop unsupported values from appearing.

Freshness, automation & failure behavior

The canonical verified publisher runs on GitHub Actions every 4 hours (best-effort scheduling), with strict tests before publication. It publishes only the verified/ artifact and never consumes the legacy dashboard's picks or seeded ratings.

A last-good inventory/quote may survive a failed fetch, but its original observation timestamp is preserved. Quotes older than 8 hours cannot price a match. Elapsed, locked, provisional or unknown-time rows remain listed without a current prematch pick. The browser also hides stale/locked probabilities between refreshes. If the inventory cannot be established at all, publication fails and preserves the last-good site.

HTTP 200 means retrieval, not data validation. Failed sources, cache use and unknown timestamps are visible below. Inspect actual refresh and deployment runs; read the latest calculation-integrity receipt.
