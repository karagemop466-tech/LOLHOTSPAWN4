📖 Methodology & Originated Line Models

This document details the quantitative methodology, data ingestion pipeline, and official sources utilized to generate independent match win probabilities and picks for Hotspawn's League of Legends pick'em game.

Core Rule: Independence from Hotspawn Crowd %

Hotspawn displays community pick percentages (e.g. "89% picked Team A") on match cards. Our pipeline strictly excludes Hotspawn crowd percentages from all probability calculations. Crowd picks reflect public sentiment, popularity biases, and streamer fandom, rather than sharp win probabilities. All probabilities originate either from external betting markets or our independent mathematical models.

⚙️ Multi-Layer Resolution Hierarchy

Every match listed on Hotspawn is processed through a strict four-tiered resolution waterfall:

Tier 1: Commercial Esportsbooks & Sharp Betting Lines (De-vigged)

When external bookmakers quote decimal odds on a series, we ingest lines via the OddsPapi LoL Network ↗ (Sport ID: 18, Series Winner market 181). This network aggregates over 100 global bookmakers, including market-making sharp books like Pinnacle, bet365, and BetOnline.

De-vigging Formula: To remove the bookmaker's overround (margin/vigorish), decimal odds $O_1, O_2$ are inverted into fair probabilities:

P(Team 1) = (1 / O₁) / [ (1 / O₁) + (1 / O₂) ]
P(Team 2) = 1 - P(Team 1)

We compute the median fair probability across all reporting bookmakers to eliminate single-book skew.

Tier 2: Prediction Markets (Polymarket Gamma API)

For featured matchups traded on decentralized prediction markets, we query the Polymarket Gamma API ↗. Traded outcome token prices directly reflect real-money equilibrium probabilities for match winners.

Tier 3: Our Own Originated Line Model (Leaguepedia Cargo Elo)

For the vast majority of matches that lack commercial sportsbook coverage (especially Tier-2, Tier-3, and European Regional Leagues), we originate our own proprietary lines.

We build a continuous Elo rating system from official League of Legends match results recorded in the Leaguepedia Cargo API ↗ (ScoreboardGames table) spanning the past 180 days.

  • Starting Rating: $R_0 = 1500$
  • K-Factor: $K = 32$
  • Raw Expected Win Probability: $E_1 = \frac{1}{1 + 10^{(R_2 - R_1)/400}}$
  • Bayesian Sample-Size Shrinkage: If a team has few recorded competitive matches, estimating ability is prone to noise. We shrink the raw probability toward 50% using sample size factor $S$:
    S = min(g₁, g₂) / [ min(g₁, g₂) + 10 ]
    P(Team 1) = 0.5 + (E₁ - 0.5) * S
    This prevents extreme probabilities on teams with insufficient historical data.

Tier 4: Transparent 50/50 Baseline for Unresolved Amateur Matches

When an amateur fixture has no external market lines and neither team has recorded games in competitive databases, we refuse to invent probabilities or hallucinate player stats. Instead, we assign an explicit 50.0% / 50.0% baseline. This is clearly flagged as Unresolved (No Edge) so players can treat it as a coin-flip.
