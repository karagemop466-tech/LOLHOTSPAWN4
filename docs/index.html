⚠️ Unresolved Picks & Limitations Analysis

Why Unresolved Matches Exist & How They Are Handled

While premier tournaments (such as Worlds 2026 or European Masters) are heavily covered by external bookmakers and prediction markets, Hotspawn's pick'em lobby features numerous regional amateur circuits (e.g. PRM 3rd Division, Tunisian Super League, Arabian League 2nd Div, 4 Nations Halloween Heist). Commercial bookmakers and prediction markets do not offer trading lines on these low-tier amateur events.

Resolution Protocol:

  • Our Originated Elo Model: If either participating team has competitive game logs recorded within the last 180 days on Leaguepedia's Cargo database, we calculate an Elo rating and shrink the probability toward 50% based on sample size.
  • 50/50 Baseline (No Edge): When neither team possesses any verifiable competitive match history in public esports databases, our system assigns an explicit 50.0% / 50.0% win probability. The nominal pick is flagged, and users are cautioned that no analytical edge exists.

Explicit Limitations for Low-Tier & Unresolved Fixtures:

  • Roster Turnover: Amateur and Tier-3 rosters change frequently between weekly matches, making past historical performance less predictive.
  • Database Coverage Gaps: Official Leaguepedia / Cargo queries capture Tier-1 and major regional leagues comprehensively, but amateur division match details may be delayed or omitted.
  • Lack of External Price Validation: Because no sharp books (like Pinnacle) price these matches, our originated model cannot be validated against external market consensus.
  • Hotspawn Metadata Incompleteness: Over 65% of lobby fixtures carry the tier flag WAITING FOR INFORMATION, requiring ongoing monitoring.
