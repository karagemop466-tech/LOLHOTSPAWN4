📖 Methodology & Originated Line Models

This document details the quantitative methodology, data ingestion pipeline, and official sources utilized to generate independent match win probabilities and picks for Hotspawn's League of Legends pick'em game.

Core Rule: Independence from Hotspawn Crowd %

Hotspawn displays community pick percentages (e.g. "89% picked Team A") on match cards. Our pipeline strictly excludes Hotspawn crowd percentages from all probability calculations. Crowd picks reflect public sentiment, popularity biases, and streamer fandom, rather than sharp win probabilities. All probabilities originate either from external betting markets or our independent mathematical models.

⚙️ Multi-Layer Resolution Hierarchy

Every available pick (fixture whose Hotspawn pick-lock time has not passed) is processed through a strict five-stage resolution waterfall. Fixtures already locked out are excluded from the board and listed on the Flagged for Review page:

Tier 1: Commercial Esportsbooks & Sharp Betting Lines (De-vigged)

When external bookmakers quote decimal odds on a series, we ingest lines via the OddsPapi LoL Network ↗ (Sport ID: 18, Series Winner market 181). This network aggregates over 100 global bookmakers, including market-making sharp books like Pinnacle, bet365, and BetOnline.

De-vigging Formula: To remove the bookmaker's overround (margin/vigorish), decimal odds $O_1, O_2$ are inverted into fair probabilities:

P(Team 1) = (1 / O₁) / [ (1 / O₁) + (1 / O₂) ]
P(Team 2) = 1 - P(Team 1)

We compute the median fair probability across all reporting bookmakers to eliminate single-book skew.

Tier 2: Prediction Markets (Polymarket Gamma API)

For featured matchups traded on decentralized prediction markets, we query the Polymarket Gamma API ↗ (LoL primary tag, id 65 — verified 2026-10-21). Traded outcome token prices directly reflect real-money equilibrium probabilities for match winners. We take the market mids exactly as reported (no adjustment). A series/moneyline market is preferred over a Game-1 market; when only a Game-1 market exists the row is flagged.

Team-name matching: markets are joined to fixtures on team names with a conservative matcher plus a small verified alias table. The only current alias is LØS (Hotspawn) = LOS (Polymarket/Kalshi), verified 2026-10-21 from the Polymarket event page lol-los-t1-2026-10-23 and Kalshi market KXLOL-WORLDS26-LOS. No unverified guesses are ever added.

Tier 3: Our Derived Line from Kalshi Tournament Futures (Ratio Model)

Where no per-match market exists but both teams have open tournament-futures markets in the same Kalshi KXLOL series ↗ (public, no key; verified 2026-10-21), we originate our own line with a transparent ratio model:

P(Team A) = f(A) / ( f(A) + f(B) )
f = futures mid price (same event, both markets active)

Each row stores both futures mids, tickers, and links so the input is auditable. This is our line, not a book line — it is labeled as a derived estimate on the board and in the Method filter.

Tier 4: Our Own Originated Line Model (Leaguepedia Cargo Elo)

For the vast majority of matches that lack commercial sportsbook coverage (especially Tier-2, Tier-3, and European Regional Leagues), we originate our own proprietary lines.

We build a continuous Elo rating system from competitive League of Legends match results recorded in the Leaguepedia Cargo API ↗ (ScoreboardGames table, community esports wiki — verified working 2026-10-21) spanning the past 180 days. The fetch is deliberately polite: one 500-game batch per refresh, merged into a persistent cache (data/raw/leaguepedia_games.json). Fandom rate-limits datacenter clients (observed 2026-10-21), so on any rate-limit or fetch error the previous cache is reused and the model degrades gracefully instead of failing the refresh. While the live cache is still filling, a seeded ratings snapshot (data/seeded_ratings.json — this same model's earlier output, added in PR #14) fills the gaps; seeded teams keep their small sample counts, so rows resolved from the seed are labeled Low confidence.

  • Starting Rating: $R_0 = 1500$
  • K-Factor: $K = 32$
  • Raw Expected Win Probability: $E_1 = \frac{1}{1 + 10^{(R_2 - R_1)/400}}$
  • Bayesian Sample-Size Shrinkage: If a team has few recorded competitive matches, estimating ability is prone to noise. We shrink the raw probability toward 50% using sample size factor $S$:
    S = min(g₁, g₂) / [ min(g₁, g₂) + 10 ]
    P(Team 1) = 0.5 + (E₁ - 0.5) * S
    This prevents extreme probabilities on teams with insufficient historical data.

Tier 5: Transparent 50/50 Baseline for Unresolved Amateur Matches

When an amateur fixture has no external market lines and neither team has recorded games in competitive databases, we refuse to invent probabilities or hallucinate player stats. Instead, we assign an explicit 50.0% / 50.0% baseline. This is clearly flagged as Unresolved (No Edge) so players can treat it as a coin-flip.
