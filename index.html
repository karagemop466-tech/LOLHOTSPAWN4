What this is. This site mirrors the LoL match list on Hotspawn's Pick'Em lobby and attaches an independently sourced win probability to every match it can. Where a public per-match market exists (Polymarket), the market mid is used directly. Where both teams carry tournament-futures prices (Kalshi / Polymarket), we originate our own line with a transparent ratio model and label it as derived. Where no verified external market exists — most low-tier regional events — the pick is intentionally left blank and the match appears on the Flagged for Review page with the limitation explained. Nothing is ever invented.
