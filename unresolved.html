Detailed Analytical Limitations

In strict adherence to project standards, we never infer or fabricate win probabilities. When a fixture lacks both commercial market liquidity and official tournament match records, our pipeline handles it transparently:

  • 50/50 Baseline (Coin-Flip): When neither team has any recorded competitive games on Leaguepedia Cargo in the past 180 days, the model assigns exactly 0.5000 / 0.5000. While Hotspawn Pick'em requires a selection to submit your ticket, our pick is nominal only and provides no statistical edge.
  • Amateur Roster Turnover: Lower-tier leagues (e.g. PRM 3rd Division, Tunisian Super League, 4 Nations Halloween Heist) experience rapid roster swaps. Even when a team name exists in historical archives, the active players may be entirely new.
  • Absence of Bookmaker Validation: Liquid markets such as Pinnacle or Polymarket aggregate sharp capital to establish efficient lines. Without external market coverage, there is no benchmark against which to calibrate.
  • Hotspawn Metadata Flags: Fixtures marked WAITING FOR INFORMATION indicate that official tournament brackets or match timings remain provisional.
