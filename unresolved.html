Best-of-1 Variance in 4 Nations & Tunisian Super League: Single-game (Bo1) amateur matches carry significantly higher draft and early-game variance than Best-of-3 (PRM, Komplettligaen, RIVALS, CELOL B) or Best-of-5 (EMEA Masters, LCP Promotion, LGC Rising Finals) series, capping realistic single-game confidence below ~88% unless a team is disqualified.
